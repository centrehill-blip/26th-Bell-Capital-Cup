CWENCH HYDRATION HOCKEY FEST

With full food & beverage service provided by our friends at the Centre Ice Restaurant, the festival also features several activities and games for the kids including giant inflatables, Sens Street Team, and so much more.

Open throughout the tournament at Bell Sensplex, CWENCH Hydration HockeyFest is a hub of activities including games, live music, pin-trading and NHL & World Junior Championship Watch Parties all day on our 16-foot giant screen!

CHAMPIONSHIP GAMES ON NHL ICE

CANADIAN TIRE CENTRE

Join teams from around the world, in the Nation’s Capital and be a part of the tournament that has seen participation from players like Connor McDavid, Shane Pinto, P.K. Subban, Mitch Marner and many other current and former NHL superstars. 

With over 600 games featuring boys and girls competing for division championships, shoot-out challenge World Junior Hockey Championships Watch Parties, and more the Bell Capital Cup is truly a hockey experience of a lifetime.
